    Case Studies — ERPNext Production Mein¶

    Lessons aap ko pieces sikhati hain. Yeh case studies dikhati hain ke jab woh pieces aik asal business se milte hain to kya hota hai — kya decisions liye gaye, kya ghaltiyan hui, aur kya result nikla. Yeh production ERPNext kaam se liye gaye anonymised patterns hain: company ki type aur size asal hai, naam badle hue hain, aur har number approximate hai — "taqreeban" aur "roughly" ka matlab yahi hai, koi measured benchmark nahi.

    Har case aik hi shape mein hai: background, problem, solution, kya ghalat hua, results, aur aap ko is se kya seekhna chahiye. Har case ke aakhri do sections hi asal point hain: solution se ideas lein, ghaltiyan parhein taake woh aap se na hon.

    Case 1 — Lahore mein aik masala manufacturer¶

    Background¶

    Aik chhoti spice manufacturer — taqreeban 20–30 log, aik factory, aik warehouse — laal mirch powder, haldi aur spice mixes pees kar pack karti hai, aur Punjab bhar ke distributors ko bechti hai. Business is point se aagay barh gaya tha jahan spreadsheet aur paper register kaam aate: mahine mein taqreeban 40–60 sales orders, 3–4 raw materials bulk mein khareede jate, aur har dispatch 1 kg aur 5 kg packs mein jati.

    Yeh wohi tarah ka business hai jo Practice Project ki factory hai — wohi flow, wohi documents, demo scale ki bajaye asal scale par.

    Problem¶

    Har haftay teen cheezein takleef deti thin:

    1. Kisi ko nahi pata tha ke stock mein asal mein kya hai. Storekeeper paper register rakhta tha; sales team spreadsheet rakhti thi. Dono aksar dinon mein alag hote, aur farq tab pata chalta jab customer ka order poora nahi ho pata. Distributor 200 packs mangwata, spreadsheet kehta ke stock mein hain, aur warehouse mein 140 milte.
    2. Quality dispatch ke baad check hoti thi, pehle nahi. Kharab batch factory ko nahi, customer ko milta tha. Aik wapas aayi hui consignment ka matlab tha aik distributor ka nuksan — aur distributors aapas mein baat karte hain.
    3. Kisi ko nahi pata tha ke aik pack ki asal cost kya hai. Kachchi mirch ki qeemat season ke saath badalti hai. Selling price andazay se tay hoti thi, aur koi nahi bata sakta tha ke aik mahine mein 1 kg pack par faida hua ya nuksan.

    Solution¶

    Core standard ERPNext tha, aur aik chhoti custom app sirf us aik cheez ke liye jo ERPNext out of the box nahi karta:

    • BOMs aur batch-wise stock ke saath manufacturing. Har finished product ka BOM bana (kachchi mirch → pisi hui powder → packed product), aur raw material aur finished goods ka har batch batch-wise track hua, taake quality problem poori mahine ki production ki bajaye aik batch tak trace ho sake. Dekhein Lesson 05 — Manufacturing aur Lesson 02 — Stock & Inventory.
    • Dispatch se pehle Quality Inspection gate. Koi Delivery Note submit nahi ho sakta tha jab tak batch ki inspection pass na ho — Server Script mein aik validation jo linked Quality Inspection check karti hai, aur submit rok deti hai agar inspection missing ho ya fail ho. Gate sirf aik validate hook hai; jo discipline woh lagata hai, wohi asal point hai. Dekhein Lesson 06 — Quality aur Lesson 05 — Server Scripts.
    • Har truck ke andar aur bahar ke liye Gate Pass. Raw material ki arrivals aur finished goods ki dispatches dono custom Gate Pass DocType se guzarti thin, jo Lesson 03 se hai, taake guard ka register aur stock ledger aakhir mein aik dosre se mil jayein.
    • Costing BOM se, andazay se nahi. BOM ki valuation rate se pack ki asal cost milti thi, aur aik simple script report har mahine use selling price se compare karti thi — pehli dafa owner dekh saka ke kis product par faida hota hai aur kis par nahi. Dekhein Lesson 08 — Reports & Print Formats.
    • Roles ne factory floor ko imandar rakha. Storekeepers Stock Entries bana sakte the lekin submit nahi kar sakte the; submit sirf production manager kar sakta tha. Dekhein Lesson 09 — Permissions & Roles.

    Kya ghalat hua¶

    1. Quality gate bahut der se lagaya gaya. Pehla version inspection ke baghair Delivery Notes submit hone deta tha, kyunke "gate go-live ke baad laga dein ge". Us gap mein aik kharab batch bahar chala gaya — wohi failure jise rokne ke liye gate bana tha. Fix yeh tha ke gate pehle din se mandatory ho, aur "baad mein laga dein ge" wala control kabhi na ho. Jo control pehle din enforce nahi hota, woh control hi nahi hai.
    2. Batch numbers haath se type hote the. Do storekeepers batch codes alag tareeqay se type karte the (BATCH-12 vs batch 12), to wohi batch reports mein do batches nazar aata tha. Fix naming series thi — code system generate karta hai, insaan kabhi type nahi karta. Field-layout rules Common Mistakes guide mein dekhein.

    Results¶

    Taqreeban, pehle teen mahino mein: register aur system ke beech stock ke disagreements haftay ki behes se nikal kar aik rare exception ban gaye; gate enforce hone ke baad koi batch passed inspection ke baghair bahar nahi gaya; aur monthly costing report ne dikhaya ke aik spice mix cost se kam par bik raha tha — us ki price usi haftay theek kar di gayi. Is mein koi jaadu nahi tha. Stock is liye theek tha kyunke har movement aik hi system se guzarti thi; quality gate is liye kaam karta tha kyunke woh submit rokta tha, sirf request nahi karta tha.

    Hum isay kaise istemal kar sakte hain¶

    Agar aap kisi manufacturer ke liye bana rahe hain, to wohi teen cheezon se shuru karein: batch-wise stock, submit rokne wala quality gate, aur costing report. Gate pehle banayein, go-live se pehle, baad mein nahi. Aur insaan ko kabhi woh identifier type na karne dein jo system generate kar sakta hai — naming series isi liye hain.

    Case 2 — Aik trading aur distribution business¶

    Background¶

    Aik distributor — taqreeban 10–15 log, aik warehouse, do delivery vans — manufacturers se packaged goods khareed kar sheher bhar ki dukanein supply karta hai. Mahine mein taqreeban 150–200 deliveries, zyada tar chhoti, zyada tar udhaar par. Business do cheezon par chalata tha: yeh janna ke har dukaan par kitna udhaar hai, aur yeh janna ke har van kahan hai.

    Problem¶

    1. Udhaar yaad-dasht se diya jata tha. Owner ko pata tha ke kaun si dukaan waqt par deti hai aur kaun si nahi — jab tak woh chhutti par na jaye, aur kisi aur ko pata na ho. Samaan un dukaanon ko bhi chala jata tha jo mahino se peechay thin.
    2. Deliveries phone call se track hoti thin. Office driver ko call karta, driver dukaan ko call karta, dukaan kehti "abhi nahi aayi". Aik delivery jo waqai ho chuki hoti, dinon tak "pending" rehti, aur us par invoice nahi ban sakta tha.
    3. Warehouse ke andar-bahar ka koi paper trail nahi tha. Samaan warehouse se baghair document ke nikal jata tha, to jab stock ghayab hota, to koi tareeqa nahi tha yeh kehne ka ke kab, ya kis ki duty par.

    Solution¶

    Taqreeban poora standard ERPNext — yeh case zyada tar standard flow ko theek tareeqay se use karne ke baare mein hai, us ke aas paas kaam karne ki bajaye:

    • Standard order-to-cash flow, shuru se end tak enforce. Sales Order → Delivery Note → Sales Invoice → Payment Entry, koi step skip nahi. Invoice sirf submitted Delivery Note se ban sakti thi, to "delivered lekin invoice nahi bani" aik category hi khatam ho gayi. Dekhein Lesson 03 — Selling aur Lesson 01 — Accounting.
    • Har Customer par credit limits. Har dukaan ko us ki payment history ke mutabiq credit limit mili, aur system limit se zyada ke naye Sales Orders rok deta tha — rule ab aik shakhs ke dimaagh mein nahi rehta tha. Overdue invoices ki daily email owner ko scheduled report se jati thi. Dekhein Lesson 11 — Scheduler & Background Jobs.
    • Delivery tracking khud Delivery Note se. Driver ka naam aur van Delivery Note par hoti, aur submitted Delivery Note hi delivery ka saboot tha — dukaan print format par sign karti, aur signed copy office wapas aati. Dekhein Lesson 08 — Reports & Print Formats.
    • Warehouse movements ke liye Gate Pass. Warehouse se nikalne wali har load ko us ki Delivery Notes se linked Gate Pass milta tha, taake guard ka register din ke end par stock ledger se mil jaye. Yeh wohi Gate Pass hai jo Lesson 03 se hai, bilkul alag business mein reuse hua.
    • Aik simple dashboard. Accounts Receivable ageing, aaj ki deliveries, aur overdue customers — aik Insights dashboard par, jo owner har subah check karta tha. Dekhein Lesson 18 — Insights.

    Kya ghalat hua¶

    1. Credit limit pehle bahut zyada set ki gayi thi. Limits purani "gut feeling" numbers se copy ki gayi thin, jo generous thin. System ne unhein perfectly enforce kiya — aur perfectly aik ghalat policy enforce ki. Fix yeh tha ke limits asal payment history se set ki jayein (average monthly payment, sab se bara mahina nahi), aur har quarter review ki jayein. Automation woh rule enforce karti hai jo aap likhte hain, woh nahi jo aap ka matlab tha.
    2. Drivers ne signed Delivery Note ki mukhalfat ki. Paper copy extra kaam lagti thi, aur do haftay tak vans baghair us ke nikaltay rahe. Fix technical nahi thi: owner ne signed copy ko driver ki daily allowance count hone ki shart bana diya. Us ke baad process chal pada. Aik system change jise koi follow na kare, woh system change hi nahi hai — insaan wala hissa plan karein, sirf DocType nahi.

    Results¶

    Taqreeban: kuch mahino mein overdue receivables numayan tor par kam ho gaye, kyunke late payers ko naye orders banana hi band ho gaye; "delivered lekin invoice nahi bani" wali list, jo pehle darjanon entries ki hoti thi, taqreeban zero ho gayi — invoice ab delivery ke usi din banti hai; aur din ke end par stock reconciliation, jo pehle storekeeper ko phone calls mein aik ghanta lagta tha, paanch minute ka kaam ban gaya — do lists ka comparison jo pehle se hi mil rahi thin.

    Hum isay kaise istemal kar sakte hain¶

    Kisi bhi trading business ke liye, standard flow hi product hai — aik mahina use as designed chalane se pehle customise na karein. Credit limits system mein honi chahiye, aik shakhs ki yaad mein nahi, aur woh payment history se aani chahiye, optimism se nahi. Aur jab aik naya step mukhalfat ka samna kare, to incentive theek karein, form nahi.

    Case 3 — Aik services company¶

    Background¶

    Aik chhoti services company — taqreeban 8–12 log — client projects karti hai: implementation kaam, support contracts, aur kabhi kabhi fixed-price jobs. Kaam spreadsheet mein track hota tha, hours chat group mein track hote the, aur invoices mahine ke end par yaad se likhe jate the. Kisi bhi waqt taqreeban 6–8 active projects, har aik ka client alag, rate alag, aur "done" ka matlab alag.

    Problem¶

    1. Kisi ko nahi pata tha ke aik project par faida hota hai ya nuksan. Hours der se ya bilkul record nahi hote the, to project ki cost aik andaza thi — aur invoice us se bhi bara andaza tha.
    2. Approvals chat mein hoti thin. Aik discount, aik free extra, scope ki tabdeeli — sab aik message mein agree hota, sab invoice ke waqt bhool jata. Client ko promise yaad rehta; company use dhoond nahi sakti thi.
    3. Clients kuch dekh nahi sakte the. Har status ka sawal phone call ya message se aata: "mera project kahan hai?", "agli invoice kab hai?" Team wohi sawal har haftay jawab deti thi.

    Solution¶

    ERPNext Projects, jis mein Timesheets sab se zyada kaam karti thin:

    • Har engagement ke liye aik Project, us ke neechay Tasks. Har kaam aik Task tha — owner aur due date ke saath — aur har ghanta Task se linked Timesheet entry tha. Project ki cost us ki Timesheet entries ka sum ban gayi — andaza khatam. Dekhein Lesson 07 — Projects.
    • Timesheets se Sales Invoices. Billable hours approved Timesheets se seedha invoice mein aate the, taake invoice us kaam se milta jo waqai hua. Fixed-price projects milestones ke khilaf invoice hote the. Dekhein Lesson 01 — Accounting.
    • Har cheez ke liye approval Workflow jo paisa kharch karti hai. Discounts, scope changes aur free kaam Sales Order par Workflow se guzarte — project lead request karta, owner approve karta — taake har promise aik document ki shakal mein ho, chat message ki nahi. Dekhein Lesson 10 — Workflows & Assignments.
    • Status aur tickets ke liye client portal. Clients portal mein login kar ke apne project ki progress dekhte aur support tickets raise karte, call karne ki bajaye. Tickets Helpdesk se guzarte, to kuch bhi chat thread mein nahi kho sakta tha. Dekhein Lesson 12 — Support & Portal aur Lesson 19 — Website & Portal.
    • Haftay ki project report. Logged hours, billed hours, aur har client par project margin — har Monday subah owner ko scheduled job se email hoti. Dekhein Lesson 11 — Scheduler & Background Jobs.

    Kya ghalat hua¶

    1. Timesheets Friday ko, yaad se bharay jate the. Team aik haftay ke hours aik baar mein log karti thi, matlab hours approximate hote — aur "approximate" hamesha upar ki taraf round hota tha. Fix 5 baje ki daily reminder aur aik rule tha: do din se zyada purani Timesheet bill hone se pehle project lead ki approval mangti hai. Hours tab accurate hue jab woh daily hue.
    2. Portal launch hua, lekin kisi client ko bataya hi nahi gaya. Portal aik mahina live raha aur taqreeban kisi ne use nahi kiya, kyunke kisi client ko bataya hi nahi gaya tha ke woh hai ya login kya hai. Fix har active client ko aik chhoti email thi — un ka login aur aik line — "aap ke project ka status ab yahan hai" — aur aik haftay mein phone calls kam ho gayi. Aik feature jise kisi ko bataya hi na jaye, woh feature hai hi nahi.

    Results¶

    Taqreeban: monthly invoice negotiation khatam ho gaya, kyunke us ki har line aik approved Timesheet ya milestone ki taraf ishara karti thi; aik project aadha guzarne par nuksan mein paya gaya, aur us ka scope end par discover karne ki bajaye usi waqt dobara negotiate hua; aur haftay ki "mera project kahan hai?" wali calls khatam ho gayi jab clients khud status dekh sakne lage. Margin report ne sab se zyada kaam kiya — us ne "kya hum faida kama rahe hain?" ko aik ehsaas se aik number mein badal diya, jise owner har Monday check karta tha.

    Hum isay kaise istemal kar sakte hain¶

    Kisi bhi services business ke liye, Timesheet source of truth hai — baaki sab kuch (invoice, margin, project status) us se derived hai, to us ki accuracy pehle protect karein. Har paisay ke decision ko Workflow se guzarein, kyunke chat mein kiya gaya promise aik dispute hai jo abhi hua nahi. Aur jab portal banayein, to launch karna aadha kaam hai; clients ko batana dosra aadha hai.

    Teenon cases mein kya common hai¶

    1. Standard flow ne zyada tar kaam kiya. In mein se kisi business ko bari custom app nahi chahiye thi. Manufacturing case ko aik gate aur aik custom DocType chahiye thi; trading case ko taqreeban kuch custom nahi chahiye tha; services case ko aik Workflow aur aik portal chahiye tha. Pehle standard flow use karein, customise baad mein.
    2. Har control jo matter karta tha, kuch rokta tha. Quality gate ne submit roka, credit limit ne order roka, approval Workflow ne discount roka. Jo control sirf warn karta hai, woh control ignore ho jata hai.
    3. Insaan wala hissa hi tay karta tha ke woh kaam kare ga ya nahi. Drivers ko signed copy le jane ke liye incentive chahiye tha; timesheets ko daily reminder chahiye thi; clients ko portal use karne ke liye email chahiye thi. DocType implementation ka aadha hissa hai — dosra aadha woh log hain jo use karte hain.
    4. Ghaltiyan sab avoid ho sakti thin — aur sab pehchani hui thin. Aik gate jo der se laga, limits jo gut feeling se copy ki gayi, hours jo yaad se log hue, aik portal jise kisi ko bataya hi nahi gaya. Agar aap yeh cases parh kar sochein "hum aisa kabhi nahi karein ge", to apne pehle go-live ke baad dobara parhein.

    Apna case study contribute karein¶

    Yeh teen cases shuruat hain, catalogue nahi. Agar aap ne ERPNext kisi asal business mein ship kiya hai — factory, dukaan, school, clinic, farm — to use isi shape mein likh kar bhej dein. Naam anonymise karein, numbers approximate aur imandari se rakhein, aur batayein ke kya ghalat hua: ghaltiyan hi sab se zyada faide mand hissa hain. Submit karne ka tareeqa CONTRIBUTING.md mein dekhein.
