    Frappe aur ERPNext — Pichle 2 Saalon Mein Kya Badla (Nov 2024 – Nov 2026)¶

    Pichle do saalon ki bari tabdeeliyan sirf — Framework/ERPNext v15 aur v16 ka daur, Studio, Flow, Insights, CRM/Helpdesk/Raven aur bari features. Har item chhota hai: kya aaya, kab, aur NexTash ke kaam ke liye ek Hum isay kaise istemal kar sakte hain line. Sab kuch official sources (Frappe Blog, Frappe Forum, GitHub releases, Frappe docs, Frappe Times) se verify kiya gaya hai, har item ke saath source link hai.

    Background (ek line mein): Nov 2024 se pehle — v13 (Apr 2021) modern Desk UI le kar aaya, v14 (Aug 2022) ne monolith ko alag apps mein baant diya (HR, Healthcare, Education…), v15 (Oct 2023) Espresso design system aur no-code Form/Workflow Builders le kar aaya, aur Frappe Cloud 2020 se sites host kar raha hai. Detail neeche se shuru hoti hai, November 2024 se.

    Support status (26 November 2026 tak)¶

    • v14 — end of life 31 Jan 2026. v15 — end 2027 tak supported. v16 — end 2029 tak supported. (Secondary summaries jo https://frappe.io/support-versions ka hawala deti hain — client ko quote karne se pehle wahan verify kar lein.)

    v15 ka daur — woh version jo window khulne par chal raha tha¶

    • v15 (Oct 2023, Nov 2024 mein abhi bhi current): Espresso UI, no-code Form Builder aur Workflow Builder, Sales Orders ke against Stock Reservation, Serial & Batch Bundle, submission ke baad invoices edit karna, 2.5x tez financial reports. Abhi bhi har mahine patch hota hai — latest v15.127.0, 25 Nov 2026. Source: https://discuss.frappe.io/t/frappe-and-erpnext-v15-release/111702
    • Hum isay kaise istemal kar sakte hain: v15 un clients ke liye safe upgrade target hai jo v16 ke naye UI se darte hain — v14→v15 ka move Form Builder demos ("meeting mein hi DocType bana kar dikhayein") aur Stock Reservation ke saath bechein, un manufacturing/trading clients ko jo v14 ki stock complaints se pareshan hain.

    November 2024 — Insights v3¶

    • Frappe Insights v3 released (Nov 2024): mukammal makeover — TypeScript frontend, ibis-framework backend, 20+ connectors, basic data warehousing. Source: https://frappe.io/times/october-2024 (announced), https://discuss.frappe.io/t/frappe-insights-tools-guide/140202/4 (November mein released)
    • Hum isay kaise istemal kar sakte hain: har go-live ke baad phase-2 sale ke tor par Insights dashboard package bechein — live data par sales, stock aur finance dashboards, managers ke liye self-service, Excel exports ki zaroorat khatam.

    January 2025 — Raven v2¶

    • Raven v2 (10 Jan 2025): workspaces (ek site par kai groups), AI bots, ERPNext integrations — chat ke andar document previews, alerts aur actions. Source: https://frappe.io/product/raven/at-frappe
    • Hum isay kaise istemal kar sakte hain: un clients ko Raven offer karein jo internal coordination ke liye WhatsApp groups mein doobay hue hain — channels ke saath ERP ke andar document alerts (invoice approvals, stock alerts).

    April 2025 — Studio open-source hua¶

    • Frappe Studio open-sourced (3 Apr 2025, Frappe Build, Bengaluru): drag-and-drop app builder, visual layouts, data sources, tez CRUD tools. Repo: https://github.com/frappe/studio. Source: https://frappe.io/blog/product-updates/product-updates-for-april-2025
    • Hum isay kaise istemal kar sakte hain: Studio sab se tez nayi service line hai — client ki custom app visually chand dinon mein banayein (sample-tracking, gate-pass, visitor register) aur fixed-price par bill karein. NexTash ke ek analyst ko is par train karein taake chhoti apps developer ka waqt lena band kar dein.

    September 2025 — Frappeverse India 2025¶

    • Frappeverse India 2025 (11–12 Sep 2025, Nehru Centre, Mumbai). Source: https://events.frappe.io
    • Hum isay kaise istemal kar sakte hain: har Frappeverse ko lead-generation aur client-touch point ke tor par use karein — us ke announcements ko ek chhoti client newsletter ("ERPNext mein kya naya hai") mein badal kar installed base se rabta banaye rakhein.

    November 2025 — v16 beta¶

    • Framework/ERPNext v16 beta (15 Nov 2025): do saalon mein pehli bari release; final 6 Dec 2025 ko Frappeverse Egypt mein planned thi. Source: https://discuss.frappe.io/t/erpnext-hrms-frappe-framework-v16-release-dates/156349
    • Hum isay kaise istemal kar sakte hain: beta window ko use kar ke apni custom apps aur top clients ki sites staging par test karein — breakage pehle pakadna ek billable "v16 readiness check" service hai.

    January 2026 — v16 final¶

    • Framework/ERPNext/Frappe HR v16 (12 Jan 2026): naya Desktop UI (desktop icons, persistent sidebar), ~2x tak tez ("Caffeine"), List View mein 50 columns tak, scrollable child tables, Chrome-based PDF converter (wkhtmltopdf ki jagah), role-based field masking, UUID document naming. ERPNext: configurable Financial Report Templates (IFRS samet), consolidated Trial Balance, forecast → MPS → MRP planning chain, batch-level stock reservations, inward subcontracting. Sources: https://frappe.io/blog/engineering/announcing-version-16, https://discuss.frappe.io/t/frappe-erpnext-and-frappe-hr-version-16-release/159053
    • Hum isay kaise istemal kar sakte hain: v16 upgrade pitches ke liye Desktop UI aur 50-column List View ko demo hook banayein — clients paanch minute mein farq dekh lete hain. Forecast → MPS → MRP chain un textile, food aur spare-parts manufacturers ko pitch karein jo abhi bhi Excel mein planning karte hain. Runtime floor barh gaya hai, is liye yeh ek-click update nahi, ek planned infrastructure upgrade hai (pehle staging) — isay ek project ke tor par bechein.

    January 2026 — Raven official ban gaya¶

    • Raven ek official Frappe app ban gaya (Jan 2026) — jis team ne isay banaya tha woh Frappe mein shamil ho gayi. Source: https://frappe.io/product/raven/at-frappe
    • Hum isay kaise istemal kar sakte hain: ab jab Raven official hai, to implementations par isay team-chat layer ke tor par bharose se propose karein — clients ki taraf se "community app" wala aitraaz khatam.

    July 2026 — Raven v3¶

    • Raven v3 (13 Jul 2026, Frappe ki timeline ke mutabiq): tez app, mobile par proper PWA, redesign, Espresso rebuild, SQLite full-text search, notifications page, reminders, scheduled messages, read receipts. Note: ek Frappeverse recap kehta hai beta 21 Aug / full release 1 Sep 2026 — dates mein farq hai, end par notes dekhein. Source: https://frappe.io/product/raven/at-frappe
    • Hum isay kaise istemal kar sakte hain: v3 ki PWA aur search Raven ko factory-floor teams ke liye workable banati hai — un clients ki implementation ke hisse ke tor par propose karein jin ka staff desk par nahi, phone par kaam karta hai.

    August 2026 — Frappeverse Mumbai 2026¶

    • Frappeverse Mumbai 2026 (21–22 Aug 2026, 1,500+ log): announcements — Frappe Suite, Frappe Cloud V2 preview, Raven V3, ERPNext v16 keynote. Sources: https://events.frappe.io/events/frappeverse-mumbai-2026, https://frappe.io/times/july-2026
    • Hum isay kaise istemal kar sakte hain: har Frappeverse ki tarah — leads aur announcements se client newsletter. Cloud V2 preview hosting-migration wali baat-cheet ka talking point hai.

    October 2026 — v16.50, polished v16¶

    • Framework/ERPNext/HR v16.50 (7 Oct 2026): navigation fix (saaf desktop, per-module dock, curated sidebar), naya print format designer (canvas + live preview), consolidated DocType Settings, revamped global search, step-based Data Import wizard, tez Kanban, List View layouts, child tables mein bulk editing. Koi breaking changes nahi — teenon apps sirf update karein. Source: https://frappe.io/blog/product-updates/announcing-framework-erpnext-and-hrms-v1650
    • Hum isay kaise istemal kar sakte hain: har nayi rollout ke liye v16.50 (early v16 nahi) recommend karein — navigation fix se clients ka main aitraaz khatam ho jata hai. Data Import wizard se go-live chhota karein (customers/suppliers addresses samet ek file mein import karein) aur naye print designer se demo mein hi client ka invoice format bana kar dikhayein.

    November 2026 — Insights v3.50 ne v2 hata diya¶

    • Frappe Insights v3.50 (17 Nov 2026): Insights v2 ko mukammal tor par hata deta hai — v2 dashboards ko pehle v3.13–v3.15.1 mein "Migrate from v2" page se migrate karna hoga (v3.15.1 released 20 Oct 2026). Source: https://docs.frappe.io/insights/articles/migrate-from-v2-to-v3
    • Hum isay kaise istemal kar sakte hain: kisi bhi v3.50 upgrade se pehle, v2-to-v3 dashboard migration ko ek scoped task ke tor par bechein — v2 dashboards wale clients isay skip nahi kar sakte.

    November 2026 — v16.60: AI core mein aa gayi¶

    • Framework/ERPNext/HR v16.60 (17 Nov 2026): Studio Framework mein merge ho gaya (bina code ke apps banayein, AI agents ke saath kaam karta hai), Frappe Flow alpha (Desk mein Frappe ka AI harness — LLMs data analyse karte hain / forms bharte hain, permission-aware, apni AI keys), Insights dashboards Desk mein embedded, behtar mobile UX, naya child-table importer, behtar report printing. Monthly releases shuru (16.70 Dec 2026 mein). ERPNext: Tally-style Day Book, 10 naye print formats, Overview dashboards ke saath redesigned Customer/Supplier forms, Projects revamp phase 1. Sources: https://frappe.io/blog/product-updates/announcing-framework-erpnext-and-frappe-hr-v1660, https://discuss.frappe.io/t/announcing-v16-60-for-frappe-framework-erpnext-and-frappe-hr/165819
    • Hum isay kaise istemal kar sakte hain: Studio se bani mini-apps ko nayi service line ke tor par offer karein aur Flow ko ek friendly client ke saath test site par pilot ke tor par ("apne data par apne ERP se poochhein"). Monthly cadence ek recurring "upgrade + what's new" retainer ko support karti hai — har 16.6x release apply karein aur client ko ek page ki summary bhejein.

    November 2026 — CRM aur Helpdesk v16.x mein renumber hue¶

    • Frappe CRM aur Helpdesk ne versioning badli (Nov 2026): continuous v1.x se Framework scheme par — v15.x Frappe v15 ko target karta hai, v16.x v16 ko (CRM v16.0.2, 5 Nov 2026; Helpdesk v16.2.1). Frappeverse 2026 par CRM: ~2.5K paid sites, ~3.5K GitHub stars; lead capture ke liye naye web Forms, Lead/Deal enrichment, Sales Hierarchy, drag-and-drop Workflow Automations. Sources: https://github.com/frappe/crm/releases, https://www.ithub.directory/crm-customer-platforms/frappe-crm (renumbering ke liye secondary)
    • Hum isay kaise istemal kar sakte hain: har naye sales project par Frappe CRM implement karein (ERPNext ka legacy CRM module nahi, jo v17 mein remove hone wala hai), aur Helpdesk un clients ke liye jin ki support shared inbox par chalti hai — unlimited agents, koi per-seat fee nahi.

    November 2026 — Frappe Cloud 2.0 public beta¶

    • Frappe Cloud 2.0 public beta (24 Nov 2026): foundation se rebuild — full root/SSH wali Virtual Machines, one-click apps, Object Storage, LLMs, firewalls aur VPN. Architecture: Pilot (sites manage karta hai, aaj Bench replacement ke tor par usable), Atlas (infrastructure), Central (customer-facing cloud). Target: Jan 2027. Bench officially archived. Source: https://discuss.frappe.io/t/frappe-cloud-2-0-is-in-public-beta/165969
    • Hum isay kaise istemal kar sakte hain: hosting-migration wale clients ko Cloud 2.0 VMs par le jayein jab yeh live ho — shared multi-tenancy ki bajaye isolated VMs downtime ki complaints ka seedha jawab hai. "Bench to Cloud 2.0" migration ko ek service ke tor par offer karein; Pilot un clients ke liye ek pitch hai jo aaj VPS par self-host kar rahe hain.

    November 2026 — monthly train: v16.61.0 aur dost¶

    • Framework v16.61.0 (24 Nov 2026): custom form button styles, maintenance mode mein by default reads ki ijazat, Letter Head Preview, Studio "Show on Desktop", Flow public agents, AI assistant images dekhta hai, Print Format role restrictions, bulk Add/Remove Roles, GitPython 3.2.1 security update. Source: https://github.com/frappe/frappe/releases/tag/v16.61.0
    • ERPNext v16.61.0 & v15.127.0, CRM v16.4.0, Helpdesk v16.2.2 (25 Nov 2026): 278 bug fixes, Stock valuation breakdown page aur auto-repost setting, draft Job Cards par Manufacturing "Return Components", POS/company defaults/Payment Entry mein permission hardening; CRM ko apne dashboard page par ek Insights dashboard mila. Sources: https://github.com/frappe/erpnext/releases/tag/v16.61.0, https://github.com/frappe/crm/releases/tag/v16.4.0, https://github.com/frappe/helpdesk/releases/tag/v16.2.2
    • Hum isay kaise istemal kar sakte hain: clients ko AMC ke under monthly train par rakhein — har mahine ka patch (jaise v16.61.0 ki permission hardening) client se rabta karne, kya badla woh batane, aur retainer bill karne ki wajah hai. Mukammal details: 2026-11-25, 2026-11-26.

    November 2026 — Builder ko AI mil gayi ("Bob")¶

    • Frappe Builder (Nov 2026): editor mein AI assistant "Bob" (slash commands, editor plugins, Frappe Cloud par AI credits); licence v1.31.0 se MIT ho gayi (pehle AGPL-3.0); latest v1.37.5, 6 Nov 2026. Source: secondary directories (iThub/exploreai) — quote karne se pehle https://github.com/frappe/builder/releases par verify karein.
    • Hum isay kaise istemal kar sakte hain: Builder se client ki marketing pages aur customer portals banayein, bina alag website project ke — kisi bhi implementation par fixed-price add-on (frappe.io khud is se bana hai).

    Dates aur figures jo double-check karne hain¶

    1. Raven v3 date: frappe.io timeline kehti hai 13 Jul 2026; Frappeverse recap kehta hai beta 21 Aug / full release 1 Sep 2026.
    2. CRM/Helpdesk renumbering: v16.0.2 (5 Nov 2026) aur v15.x/v16.x scheme ek secondary directory (iThub) se hai, Frappe ki announcement se nahi — https://github.com/frappe/crm/releases par verify karein.
    3. v16.60 "2,500+ bugs fixed": secondary summaries mein aata hai; Frappe ka v16.60 blog line-by-line check nahi kiya gaya.
    4. Support dates (v14 EOL 31 Jan 2026; v15 end 2027 tak; v16 end 2029 tak): sirf secondary summaries se verify hue jo https://frappe.io/support-versions ka hawala deti hain.
    5. Insights v3 ki exact tag date (Nov 2024) aur Builder v1.37.5 / "Bob" details: secondary sources se hain; https://github.com/frappe/insights/releases aur https://github.com/frappe/builder/releases par verify karein.
