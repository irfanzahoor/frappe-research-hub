# Manufacturing — Frappe / ERPNext Apps

> [Apps Catalog](../apps-catalog.md) ka hissa — is category mein 4 apps hain. Har app mukhtasar hai: ye kya karta hai, kyun faida-mand hai, NexTash ke kaam ke liye ek **Hum isay kaise istemal kar sakte hain** line, aur GitHub link + install command. Pehle staging par test karein.


Note: ERPNext core mein pehle se multi-level BOMs, work orders, job cards, Production Plan/MRP, subcontracting aur quality inspection hain — ye apps industry ya planning depth add karte hain.

### Apparelo — garment manufacturing workflow
- **What/why:** cutting/stitching/packing stage workflows jo generic work orders model nahi karte — garment clients ke liye industry layer.
- **Hum isay kaise istemal kar sakte hain:** garment/textile exporters (Lahore, Faisalabad) ko stage workflows custom-build karne ki bajaye ye pitch karein — ye generic manufacturing sale ko industry-specific sale mein badal deta hai.
- **GitHub:** https://github.com/aerele/apparelo — `bench get-app https://github.com/aerele/apparelo`

### AuMMS — gold manufacturing management
- **What/why:** gold/jewellery manufacturing (purity, wastage, making charges) — Pakistan/India/Gulf mein ERPNext ka ek asal niche.
- **Hum isay kaise istemal kar sakte hain:** Pakistan aur Gulf ke gold/jewellery manufacturers ko offer karein — ek aisa niche jahan kam ERP vendors compete karte hain.
- **GitHub:** https://github.com/efeone/aumms — `bench get-app https://github.com/efeone/aumms`

### ERPNext Frepple Integration — advanced production planning / APS
- **What/why:** ERPNext ko Frepple ke saath pair karta hai, jo open-source advanced planning & scheduling engine hai, built-in MRP se aage constraint-based planning ke liye.
- **Hum isay kaise istemal kar sakte hain:** bari factories ko phase-2 sale ke tor par offer karein jo ERPNext ke apne Production Plan se aage nikal gayi hain.
- **GitHub:** https://github.com/msf4-0/ERPNext-Frepple-Integration — `bench get-app https://github.com/msf4-0/ERPNext-Frepple-Integration`

### Agriculture — agriculture domain for ERPNext (official Frappe)
- **What/why:** agri-business clients ke liye crop/livestock management domain, usi ERP ke andar.
- **Hum isay kaise istemal kar sakte hain:** sugar mills, dairy farms aur seed companies ko offer karein — ek bara Pakistani segment jise zyada tar ERP vendors ignore karte hain.
- **GitHub:** https://github.com/frappe/agriculture — `bench get-app https://github.com/frappe/agriculture`
