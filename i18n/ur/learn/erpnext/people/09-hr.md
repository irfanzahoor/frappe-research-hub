# HR — Employees, Leave, Attendance, Payroll, Shifts

## Ye kya karta hai

HR business ka people side chalata hai: yahan kaun kaam karta hai, kab
chhutti par hai, aaya ya nahi, aur usay kya pay milti hai. Ek **Employee**
ko leave allocate hoti hai, wo us ke liye apply karta hai, present ya
absent mark hota hai, **Shift** par kaam karta hai, aur **Payroll Entry**
se generate hone wali **Salary Slip** se pay pata hai. v16 mein HR module
alag **Frappe HR** app (hrms) hai — Employee master khud abhi bhi ERPNext
core mein hai, aur HRMS us par build karta hai.

## Core DocTypes

| DocType | Ye kya hai |
|---|---|
| Employee | Master: naam, company, department, designation, date of joining, reports-to. |
| Leave Type | Chhutti ki qisam: Annual, Sick, Casual — apne rules ke saath. |
| Leave Allocation | Employee ko aik period ke liye leave days deta hai. |
| Leave Application | Request: dates, leave type, approver. Submit par leave ledger mein post hoti hai. |
| Leave Ledger Entry | Leave ki aik movement. Automatically banti hai — **isay kabhi haath se insert mat karo**. |
| Attendance | Aik din ka record: Present, Absent, Half Day, On Leave. |
| Employee Checkin | Aik punch: in ya out, time ke saath (biometric ya manual entry se). |
| Shift Type | Aik shift: start, end, late-entry aur early-exit rules. |
| Shift Assignment | Employee ko aik period ke liye shift par lagata hai. |
| Holiday List | Saal ki chhuttiyan, har company ya location ke liye. |
| Salary Structure | Pay design: earnings aur deductions (basic, allowances, tax, provident fund). |
| Salary Structure Assignment | Employee ko aik date se structure assign karta hai. |
| Salary Slip | Aik employee ki aik mahine ki pay: gross, deductions, net. |
| Payroll Entry | Payroll run: employees ke set ke liye Salary Slips generate karta hai. |
| Expense Claim | Employee ka claim (travel, fuel) approval aur payment ke liye. |

## Shuru se akhir tak flow

```text
Setup:   Departments → Holiday List → Leave Types → Shift Types → Salary Structures
Hire:    Employee → Leave Allocation → Shift Assignment → Salary Structure Assignment
Daily:   Employee Checkin → Attendance (auto-marked from checkins)
Leave:   Leave Application (submit) → balance drops → Attendance shows On Leave
Pay:     Payroll Entry → Salary Slips → Payment Entry (bank) → ledger
Check:   Leave Balance, Attendance, Salary Register reports
```

- **Leave Allocation** days deta hai; **Leave Application** unhein kharch
  karti hai. Allocation nahi, to application nahi — balance negative ho
  jata hai aur report ye dikhati hai.
- **Attendance** **Employee Checkin** (biometric ya manual punches) se
  mark ho sakti hai ya direct enter ki ja sakti hai. Checkins audit trail
  hain; attendance wo record hai jo payroll parhta hai.
- **Payroll Entry** run hai: period aur employees choose karo, aur ye
  Salary Slips generate karta hai. Slip har employee ke liye har mahine
  aik hoti hai — submitted slip ko kabhi edit mat karo, cancel karo aur
  dobara generate karo.
- **Salary Structure** wo jagah hai jahan pay policy rehti hai: basic pay,
  house rent, medical, tax aur provident fund components ke tor par.
  Policy structure mein badlo, individual slips mein nahi.

## Amli misaal — aik factory, aik mahina

Aik spice factory mein do shifts par 45 staff hain:

1. **Employees** department, designation aur joining date ke saath bane.
   Saal ke liye **Holiday List**, **Shift Types** Morning (8–4) aur Evening
   (4–12), har worker ke liye **Shift Assignments**.
2. Daily, workers punch in karte hain. **Employee Checkin** records
   **Attendance** feed karte hain — Present, Late, ya Absent.
3. Aik worker 3 din annual leave ke liye apply karta hai. **Leave
   Application** approve hoti hai; balance 12 se 9 ho jata hai; wo din On
   Leave dikhate hain.
4. Mahine ke akhir mein: **Payroll Entry** 45 **Salary Slips** generate
   karta hai. Late days salary structure ke hisaab se deduct hote hain;
   leave days paid hote hain. Slips submit hoti hain aur bank se aik
   **Payment Entry** mein pay hoti hain.
5. **Salary Register** har employee ke liye gross, deductions aur net
   dikhata hai — wahi report jo accountant file karta hai.

## Asli use case

60 logon ki aik company attendance register mein aur payroll Excel mein
chalati thi. Leave balances HR head ki yaad mein rehte thay, aur salary
day dobara check karne mein teen din lagte thay. HR ke baad, checkins
attendance automatically mark karte hain, leave balances sab ko nazar
aate hain, aur payroll run aik dopahar mein ho jata hai — Salary Register
audit record ke tor par.

**Hum isay kaise istemal kar sakte hain:** HR ko Accounting ke baad dusra
module becho kisi bhi client ko jis ke 20+ staff hon — attendance + leave
+ payroll wo package hai jis ka dard har growing business mehsoos karta
hai. Biometric checkin sync aik chhota fixed-price add-on hai (apps
catalog ki HR entries dekho).

## Aam ghaltiyan

- **Allocation ke baghair leave.** Zero balance ke khilaaf applications
  negative balances aur saal ke akhir mein arguments banati hain.
- **Edited slips se payroll.** Submitted Salary Slip aik record hai —
  cancel karo aur dobara generate karo, kabhi edit mat karo.
- **Koi Shift Assignment nahi.** Is ke baghair, checkins ke paas compare
  karne ke liye kuch nahi hota aur "late" ka koi matlab nahi hota.
- **Salary policy slips mein, structures mein nahi.** 45 slips mein type
  kiya gaya wahi allowance ghalat hone ke 45 chances hain. Isay structure
  mein daalo.
- **Attendance daily haath se mark karna.** Checkins istemal karo. 45
  logon ke liye manual attendance aik full-time job hai aur disputes ki
  wajah hai.
